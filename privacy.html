Privacy Policy

This page explains what personal information may be processed when you visit this portfolio, use its external links, or contact me directly.

1. Who is responsible for this website

This is the personal portfolio of Francesco Marrocco. For privacy-related questions or requests, you can contact me at frmarrocco03@gmail.com.

2. Information processed when you visit

The website itself does not provide user accounts, registrations, contact forms, advertising, or behavioural analytics. Its own code does not intentionally include analytics or advertising trackers.

As with most websites, the hosting and network infrastructure used to deliver the site may automatically process technical request information such as an IP address, browser or user-agent information, requested pages, timestamps, and security or error logs. This technical processing is generally necessary to deliver, protect, and maintain the website.

3. Cookies and tracking

This portfolio does not intentionally set profiling or advertising cookies and does not use its own cookie-based analytics. Third-party websites that you choose to open from this site may use their own cookies or tracking technologies under their own privacy policies.

4. External links and services

The website contains links to third-party services, including professional, academic, social, code-hosting, and document-sharing platforms. When you follow one of these links, the destination service is responsible for its own processing of personal data. You should review the privacy information provided by that service if you want to understand how it handles your data.

5. Email contact

If you contact me by email, I may process the information you provide, such as your email address, name, message content, and any attachments, in order to read and respond to your request and maintain necessary correspondence.

I do not use contact information received through personal correspondence for advertising or sell it to third parties.

6. Retention

Personal correspondence is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose of the conversation, record-keeping, security, or any applicable legal obligation. Technical logs, where generated by hosting or infrastructure providers, are subject to the retention practices of those providers.

7. Your privacy rights

Where applicable under data-protection law, including the GDPR, you may have rights concerning your personal data, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection. The availability of a specific right depends on the circumstances and legal basis of the processing.

You may contact me at the email address above to exercise an applicable right. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection authority.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the website changes, for example if analytics, forms, embedded services, or other data-processing features are added. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
